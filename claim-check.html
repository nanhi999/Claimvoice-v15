Claim Check — ClaimVoice

A simple Claim Check

1. What happened?

Accident, theft, partial theft, fire, flood, vandalism, total loss/CTL or another event?

2. What is the insurer saying?

Rejected, delayed, partly settled, investigation pending, additional documents or another reason?

3. Is the reason written?

Keep the exact wording and any policy clause identified.

4. What evidence do you have?

Photos, videos, police records where relevant, estimates, invoices, correspondence and timeline.

5. Who has done what?

Was a surveyor appointed? Was an investigation conducted? What remains pending?

6. What do you want next?

Clarification, reconsideration, assessment explanation, payment or formal grievance?

Vehicle recovered after theft?

If your stolen vehicle has been recovered, tell ClaimVoice so you can see the immediate security warning and recovery guide.

⚠️ Your vehicle may have a security risk after recovery

If the vehicle was stolen, its locks, ignition, keys or other security components may have been compromised.

Get the locks, ignition and relevant security system professionally checked immediately. If a component is compromised, consider replacing it.

  • Aftermarket: keep the genuine purchase bill/invoice and job card.
  • Authorised service centre: keep the invoice and job card; the work may also be supported by the vehicle's authorised service history.

This is a practical security precaution, not a statement that IRDAI universally mandates replacement of locks after every theft recovery. If a later theft occurs, the insurer may ask questions about the circumstances and precautions taken after recovery.
