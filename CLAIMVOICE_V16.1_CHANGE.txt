ARTICLE SAFETY/ACCURACY POINTS:
• Court release and superdari are explained as general processes; exact court/police requirements can vary.
• Recovery is not presented as an automatic cancellation or automatic approval of a theft claim.
• Non-traceable/untraced reporting is explained as a status that must be updated when recovery occurs.
• No claim is made that IRDAI universally mandates replacing locks after every theft recovery. The article recommends a professional security check and replacement of compromised components as a practical precaution.
• If the theft claim was already paid, the article tells the reader to notify the insurer and not dispose of the vehicle until rights are clarified.
